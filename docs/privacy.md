# Privacy and real publishing effects

[العربية](privacy.ar.md) · [Main guide](../README.md)

## The public package

This repository contains reusable instructions, blank configuration/ledger templates, invented reasoning scenarios, bilingual guides and public promotional artwork. It contains no original source conversations, real account bindings, actual campaigns, recipients or private screenshots. Approved author contacts are `eyoussef-builds`, `hello@eyoussef.me` and `eyoussef.me`.

The workflow was generalized from a real process; its private evidence is not included. Installation paths such as `~/.agents/skills/` are generic user conventions, not the creator's machine details. The skill is instruction-only: no hidden account database, browser extension, bundled executable scheduler or credential store is provided.

## Your private working state

Keep your installed `references/local-profile.md`, filled campaign ledger, platform object IDs/admin URLs, account handles, browser state, queue screenshots and recipients outside public repositories/releases. The blank templates are safe starting structures; filling them produces private operational records. The supplied `.gitignore` is only a convenience and cannot protect already committed or force-added files.

Use the signed-in browser or an authorized connector through the host's supported tool. Do not export cookies, credentials or tokens to enable automation. Verify the actual actor/destination rather than inferring it from a remembered ID or someone else's example.

## What data moves when used

- The assistant's model processes repository material and any working evidence read into its context under the host/provider's settings.
- Image services receive production prompts/references. Give them approved public visuals, not private dashboards or inboxes.
- Scheduling uploads the chosen copy and images to the selected social account/platform, where they may publish later automatically.
- Enabling a requested comment rule configures future platform replies/messages to people who trigger that rule, within the saved channel/scope. Verify those effects before saving.
- A cross-chat handoff sends its text and referenced artifact details to that chat only when authorized. It does not authorize unrelated replies, DMs or other account actions.

The skill does not claim offline-only processing. Preparing files alone does not post content or send messages; actual scheduling and rule changes require the user's relevant authority and tool access.

## Review and report honestly

Inspect every caption, keyword, URL, image, actor and local timestamp before submission. Check native queues and reopen saved rules afterward. An enabled rule is not an end-to-end delivery test, a draft is not scheduled, and a scheduled post is not yet published. Keep raw verification evidence private while giving the user useful proof locally.

When publishing this skill or your own derivative, export only reusable files into a clean reviewed package. Exclude real profiles/ledgers and private history. Review image pixels/metadata, file contents, archive member names, Git identity and reachable history before and after publication. The skill's procedural privacy guidance is not a guarantee of universal anonymization.
